# Search tips

- Enter one or more keywords; use quotation marks to search a phrase (e.g. “Mississippi River”)
- To see a result in our digital collections (e.g. full diary, set of letters), click the image thumbnail to view the page at our digital collections site, then click the "Open book" link below the page image
- Would you prefer to download and search data as a JSON file? [See our data download page](https://gitlab.com/newberry-library/transcribe/-/tree/main/bulk_data)
